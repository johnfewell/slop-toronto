    How to explore Toronto

    Find & go

    • / Search places, streets, districts, or a kind of place: “hotel”, “bank”, “pharmacy”
    • Directions from anywhere to anywhere, by car, on foot or by bicycle
    • Start for turn-by-turn guidance, or Tour Drive and the guide takes you there
    • M The map: click any spot for directions, Go there to jump

    Touch controls

    • Joystick (bottom left): push up to go, down to brake or reverse, sideways to steer
    • Drag anywhere else to look around · pinch to zoom
    • Boost to speed up · Jump / Up / Down appear in the modes that use them
    • The buttons at the bottom switch between Walk, Drive, Cycle and Fly

    Walk 1

    • WS or ↑↓ Walk
    • AD or ←→ Turn
    • Shift Run · Space Jump

    Drive 2

    • W Accelerate · S Brake / reverse
    • AD Steer · Space Handbrake
    • Shift Floor it · traffic drives on the right
    • B Horn

    Cycle 3

    • W Pedal · S Brake
    • AD Lean & turn
    • Shift Sprint · B Bell

    Fly 4

    • WS Pitch · AD Yaw
    • Space/E Up · Q/C Down
    • Shift Boost

    Look & view

    • Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom
    • V Next camera · 5–0 Cameras & sights
    • H Clean view · ? This help
    • N Sound on / off
