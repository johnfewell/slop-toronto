How it works: search, route, ride

Search like a map app. Type a place, a street, a neighbourhood or just “café”. Suggestions appear as you type, nearest first, typos forgiven.

Plan any route. From where you stand, or from anywhere, to anywhere. By car (one-way streets obeyed), on foot or by bicycle, with every turn spelled out. Try directions to Union Station.

Go, or let the guide drive. Follow the blue line on the road with live turn-by-turn guidance, or switch on Tour Drive and the guide takes you there, stopping at red lights.

What's real, and what's made up

Buildings. Every footprint and height from the City of Toronto's 3D Massing. The façades are the engine's own: no photographs.

Streets. OpenStreetMap's roads with their lanes and one-way streets, laid on the City's edge-of-roadway map, signals where they are mapped.

Trees & street furniture. Street trees, fire hydrants and bike rings where the City's open data puts them.

Water & light. The lake, the harbour and the shoreline from OpenStreetMap; the terrain from SRTM. The sky is Toronto's: the sun where it stands now, and the cloud, fog, rain or snow Pearson reports.
